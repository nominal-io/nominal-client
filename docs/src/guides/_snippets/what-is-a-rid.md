:::{note}

All Nominal primitives (e.g. Datasets, Runs, Workbooks, and Checks) have a unique identifier called a "Resource ID". Resource IDs may be referred to as "RIDs", or "IDs", throughout the platform. 

They can be obtained from a primitive's detail page (or URL) and have a format that looks like `ri.catalog.cerulean-staging.dataset.e5ede17b-05f9-404d-aaf5-ba85c99761a2`.
:::
