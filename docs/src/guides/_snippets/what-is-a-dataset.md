:::{note}

A *Dataset* is a persistent data source containing channels and their data.
Multiple file uploads and streams can append to it, including video and text log channels.
A returned `DatasetFile` represents one uploaded file; use it to track ingestion
and inspect that file's bounds separately from the dataset's overall bounds.

Head over to the [Datasets page](https://app.gov.nominal.io/data-sources?sidebar=allDatasets)
to see your organization's most recently uploaded Datasets.
:::
